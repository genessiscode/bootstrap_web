About TravelPearl: Our Story

Pioneering Sustainable Hospitality in Sri Lanka

Founded with a passion for authentic discovery, TravelPearl connects global explorers to the rich cultural heritage, misty tea hills, and pristine coastlines of our island nation.

Our Story & Values Meet the Team

Our Heritage and Vision

Dedicated to ethical exploration, community empowerment, and unforgettable island journeys.

Our Mission

To deliver frictionless, verified, and personalized hospitality across Sri Lanka while protecting fragile ecosystems and creating lasting economic value for regional hosts.

  • 100% verified independent local hosts
  • Fair-wage policies for guides and drivers
  • Carbon-neutral expedition planning

Our Vision

To be South Asia's leading sustainable destination management platform, setting gold standards in eco-resort curation, heritage preservation, and digital traveler convenience.

  • Certified green accommodation network
  • Integrated digital booking and live support
  • Community tourism development grants

Our Promise

Direct, transparent pricing with zero surprise surcharges, 24/7 dedicated local concierge support, and flexible booking protection for complete peace of mind.

  • Guaranteed best rate match
  • Dedicated on-trip emergency response
  • Transparent cancellation policies

Our Four Foundational Pillars

How we ensure every journey delivers unmatched authenticity, comfort, and positive impact.

Authentic Heritage

Direct partnerships with UNESCO sites, ancient monastery preservation trusts, and licensed native guide lecturers.

Eco-Certified Stays

150+ audited boutique estates, eco-villas, and wildlife lodges adhering to strict waste and water recycling protocols.

Guest Safety First

Fully insured vehicles, GPS-tracked chauffeur logistics, and accredited first-responder tour managers on call 24/7.

Local Community Value

Over 85% of trip expenditure directly supports regional homestays, rural spice farms, and artisan handicraft guilds.
