Project Role & Editorial Statement

As Member 1 and the primary page editor for TravelPearl, my mandate was to establish a high-end, uncluttered, and strictly conforming design language inspired by modern European and island hospitality web standards.

I structured the core landing page (index.html) with 8 comprehensive sections: Hero Search Engine, Four Core Feature Pillars, Featured Resort Accommodations, Popular Destinations, Transparent Pricing Table, Booking Request Form, Verified Guest Reviews, and Interactive FAQ Accordion.
