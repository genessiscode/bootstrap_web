Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries regarding reservations, check-in, and island transfers.

Most standard reservations enjoy free cancellation up to 48 hours prior to the scheduled check-in date. Certain peak holiday and luxury villa bookings may require a 7-day notice period.

Airport transfers are complimentary for stays of 3 nights or more in select premium packages. For other reservations, private air-conditioned transport can be arranged for a modest partner surcharge.

Sri Lanka enjoys dual monsoon seasons, making it a year-round destination. The south and west coasts (Galle, Bentota) and the hill country are ideal from December to April, while the east coast (Trincomalee, Arugam Bay) shines from May to September.

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) as well as direct bank wire transfers. Final bill settlements can also be made at the hotel reception in USD or LKR.
